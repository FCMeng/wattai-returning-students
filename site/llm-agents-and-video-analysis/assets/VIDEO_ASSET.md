# Instructor video asset

Lecture 3 uses the packaged NASA Scientific Visualization Studio clip
`assets/nasa_three_pacific_hurricanes.mp4`. Its official source and usage
guidance are recorded in `nasa_video.json`.

The accompanying `video_ground_truth.json` separates visible evidence from
NASA metadata. During class, emphasize that a human reference supports review;
it is not an automatic claim that every description is correct.

If an instructor substitutes another clip, record its title, creator, source,
license or usage terms, access date, checksum, duration, and human annotations.
Do not distribute media unless its terms permit redistribution.
