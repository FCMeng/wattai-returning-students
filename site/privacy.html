Analytics and Privacy

What the course website measures and what it does not collect.

Aggregate website analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate course usage and page performance. Reports may include page paths, referring sites, country-level location, device and browser categories, and page-load performance.

The course operator does not use this analytics service to collect names, email addresses, Clemson user IDs, API keys, assignment answers, or individual student identities. Statistics may be incomplete when a browser, extension, or network blocks the analytics beacon.

Cloudflare states that Web Analytics collects a minimal amount of information and does not track individual end users across its customers’ websites. Geographic and referral reports are approximate: VPNs can change the apparent country, and some browsers and applications do not provide a referrer.

For technical details, see Cloudflare’s data collection and analytics dimensions documentation.

Material feedback

Lesson and homework pages link to a Clemson Qualtrics feedback form. The link identifies the course material and page, not the student. The form includes name and Clemson email fields that students may choose to complete; those fields are not required.
