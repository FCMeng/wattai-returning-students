Before you begin

  1. Download and unzip the complete course.
  2. Install requirements.txt in a fresh environment.
  3. Request a suitable Palmetto 2 GPU and confirm internet access.
  4. Open notebooks/00_setup_and_diagnostics.ipynb.

Individual notebooks are useful for reading, but the complete bundle is required for the relative data, media, adapter, and helper paths to work.
